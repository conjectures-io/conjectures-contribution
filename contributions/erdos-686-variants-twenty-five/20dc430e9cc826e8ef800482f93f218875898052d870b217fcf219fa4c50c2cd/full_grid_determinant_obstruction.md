# Full transport support: an exact polynomial obstruction and rigid integral margins

2026-10-08. Focused mathematical audit; no literature search or new witness search. The full-support case remains unresolved. The useful output is a precise explanation of why two tempting algebraic extensions do not, by themselves, strengthen the higher-contact method.

## 1. All-grid contact is exactly divisibility by the original product

Let P(T)=product_(i=1)^k(T+i), F(X,Y)=P(Y)-25P(X), and

    A=Q[X,Y]/(F).

For the k^2 rational grid points (-i,-j), put m_ij=(X+i,Y+j) in A. Let R>=1. Then, for any polynomial G,

    G has branch contact at least R at every grid point
    if and only if
    G belongs to (P(X)^R) in A.                      (1)

Equivalently, there exist rational polynomials H,K such that

    G=P(X)^R H+F K.                                 (2)

This applies to a polynomial produced as a determinant or a Pluecker minor just as it applies to any other polynomial. Uniform full-grid divisibility is not merely at the degree threshold: it is an exact multiple of the original product divisor on the curve.

### Proof of (1)

All grid points are smooth, since P'(-j) is nonzero. At (-i,-j) the formal branch may be parametrized by T=X+i, with Y+j a power series with rational coefficients and nonzero linear term. Its truncation modulo T^R gives a surjective homomorphism A -> Q[T]/(T^R).

The kernel is m_ij^R. Indeed, in the Artinian local ring A/m_ij^R the equation F has linear term P'(-j)(Y+j)-25P'(-i)(X+i). Thus the maximal ideal is generated by X+i, by Nakayama's lemma; equivalently, successive substitution expresses Y+j as a polynomial in X+i modulo that power. This shows dimension at most R. The displayed surjection shows dimension at least R, and hence is an isomorphism. Thus order-R branch contact is exactly membership in m_ij^R, not merely a statement about set-theoretic vanishing.

The maximal ideals m_ij are pairwise comaximal. Furthermore,

    A/(P(X)) = Q[X,Y]/(P(X),P(Y)) = product_(i,j) Q,

because P splits into distinct rational linear factors. Hence the ideal (P(X)) is the intersection, and therefore the product, of all m_ij. Comaximality also holds for their powers. It follows that

    intersection_(i,j) m_ij^R
      = product_(i,j) m_ij^R
      = (product_(i,j) m_ij)^R
      = (P(X)^R),

proving (1).

### Exact degree consequence

Reduce polynomials modulo the monic polynomial F in Y. This does not increase total degree and gives a unique representative of Y-degree less than k. If G is nonzero in A and has total degree at most D, equation (2) becomes the literal polynomial equality

    G_reduced=P(X)^R H_reduced,

since the right side also has Y-degree less than k. Therefore

    D >= kR+degree(H_reduced) >= kR.                 (3)

At D=kR the only reduced possibilities are constant multiples of P(X)^R. At an exact integer solution, evaluating (2) yields G(n,m)=P(n)^R H(n,m); after clearing the fixed rational denominator of H this divisibility is already supplied by P(n)^R. Such a construction cannot produce the missing degree saving. This does not rule out nonuniform contact, rational functions with separately controlled denominators, or arguments using more than this divisibility.

The nontrivial kernels at the threshold are exactly the spans of P(X) and P(X)^2, respectively. Ranks are certified by elimination modulo 101 (a lower bound on rational rank), together with the explicit integer nullvectors (the matching upper bound). Thus this check does not depend on floating-point rank decisions.

## 2. The natural constant Pluecker identity does not acquire a common divisor

Every edge group Q_ij divides both n+i and m+j, hence divides

    D_ij=d+j-i,   d=m-n.

The difference matrix D has rank at most two, and its 2 by 2 minors have the exact constant value

    D_ij D_hl - D_il D_hj = (h-i)(l-j).             (4)

Writing D_ij=Q_ij c_ij turns (4) into

    Q_ij Q_hl c_ij c_hl - Q_il Q_hj c_il c_hj
      = (h-i)(l-j).                                (5)

This looks promising because the right side is bounded independently of n. But for four distinct cells the two displayed Q-products are coprime. They do not divide both terms, and thus their product does not divide the constant. The remaining c_ij are positive integers with no fixed upper bound: the known information gives only c_ij=(d+j-i)/Q_ij. In a putative balanced full grid, Q_ij is on the scale n^(1/k), so these residuals are on the scale n^(1-1/k), not constants.

Equation (4) is an identity for every pair of consecutive blocks, whether or not their product ratio is 25. Equation (5) is its exact reformulation and supplies no new valuation contradiction by itself. This does not rule out a future simultaneous argument for many identities (5); it identifies the missing step precisely: control of their unbounded cofactors or a new shared divisor.

## 3. Product-margin transformations give no integral descent at fixed n,m

The whole-product normalization gives finite choices of residuals a_i,b_j and

    R_i=(n+i)/a_i=product_j Q_ij,
    S_j=(m+j)/b_j=product_i Q_ij,

with all Q_ij pairwise coprime. Under precisely this coprimality condition,

    Q_ij=gcd(R_i,S_j).                              (6)

Indeed, a prime occurs in one cell only, so it occurs in both the selected row and selected column exactly when it lies in their intersection; its exponent is then the same in both margins. This proves (6) prime by prime. Therefore the edge matrix is uniquely determined by the integer margins. It is not an additional collection of free integral parameters once n,m and the residuals are fixed.

Over positive rationals, a four-cell transformation multiplying Q_ij,Q_hl by t and dividing Q_il,Q_hj by t preserves the row and column products. Under pairwise coprime integral entries, it is trivial: write t=a/b in lowest terms. Integrality forces a to divide both Q_il and Q_hj and b to divide both Q_ij and Q_hl, so a=b=1. More general margin-preserving moves are excluded by the uniqueness (6).

Thus these familiar multiplicative moves cannot supply an integral descent at fixed margins. A descent changing n,m must also preserve consecutiveness and the exact multiplier-25 equation; the factor matrix alone does not provide one. Finite residual choices likewise do not make the prime set fixed, so they do not turn (5) into a finite prescribed-prime equation.

## Verification and scope

`full_grid_determinant_obstruction.py/.json` checks the contact matrices and explicit nullvectors, the constant minors for a finite integer diagnostic, and the gcd-margin identity on a full pairwise-coprime 5 by 5 matrix. These are controls for the displayed algebraic proofs, not witness searches. Nothing is claimed about the existence of a target solution from those diagnostic matrices. No Lean proof or new height bound for full support is produced.

The remaining opening is genuinely outside uniform polynomial contact: for example, a simultaneous cofactor bound in (5) that uses the exact product equation, or a denominator-controlled rational auxiliary. Neither is established here.
